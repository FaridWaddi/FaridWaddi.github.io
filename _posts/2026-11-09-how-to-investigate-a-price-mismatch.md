Imagine an online store where the product page displays a price of $25, but the shopping cart shows $30 for the same item.

Is this a UI defect, a calculation defect, or a data consistency issue?

The answer depends on the root cause, but the first step is to investigate the difference.

Useful checks include:

- Compare the product page price with the cart price.
- Check whether a discount or additional charge applies.
- Verify the quantity and subtotal.
- Confirm that the displayed total matches the expected calculation.

A small price mismatch can affect customer trust and lead to checkout problems.

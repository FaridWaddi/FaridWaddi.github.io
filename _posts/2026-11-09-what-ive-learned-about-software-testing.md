One thing I've realized is that testing is more than checking whether a website works.

A QA tester needs to think about different situations, understand what the user expects, and look for problems before they affect the user.

I've been focusing on manual testing, writing test cases, executing them, and reporting defects. I enjoy the process of finding problems and understanding why they happen.

I'm looking forward to improving my skills through more practice.
